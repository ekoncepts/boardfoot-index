Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $7.50/bf (4/4) at The Wood Yard in Concord, GA; the highest was $18.40/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $13.07 vs surfaced median $15.00 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
  • 8/4 median $15.00 vs 4/4 median $10.42 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • Only 12 prices from 5 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
