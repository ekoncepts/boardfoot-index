Mahogany price per board foot

How much does mahogany lumber cost? Across 54 publicly posted dealer prices checked in October 2026, mahogany has a median of $9.10 per board foot, ranging $5.69–$18.40. Data comes from dealer price sheets in FL, GA, IA, IL, MD, MI, NC, NH, NJ, OH, ON, TX, WA, WI; every price links to its source.

    Buying tips from the data

    • The lowest posted price we found was $5.69/bf (4/4, rough (Select & Better; African mahogany); posted CAD $7.995/bf converted to USD at 1.4057 CAD/USD (Bank of Canada, 2026-10-21)) at Peacock Lumber in Oshawa, ON; the highest was $18.40/bf.
    • Rough-sawn median $8.80 vs surfaced median $10.88 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
    • 8/4 median $9.95 vs 4/4 median $8.75 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
    • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
    • Peacock Lumber posts prices in Canadian dollars. We converted them to US dollars at the Bank of Canada rate for October 21, 2026 (1.4057 CAD per USD); the original CAD price is in the Surfacing column, and the US-dollar figure moves with the exchange rate.
    • Only 54 prices from 13 states plus Ontario so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
