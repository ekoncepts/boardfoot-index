Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $7.50/bf (4/4) at Cygnus Lumber in Virginia Beach, VA; the highest was $15.00/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $13.23 vs surfaced median $15.00 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
  • 8/4 median $10.50 vs 4/4 median $9.47 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • Only 16 prices from 6 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
