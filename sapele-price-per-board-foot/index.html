Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $6.37/bf (4/4, rough (Select & Better); posted CAD $8.95/bf converted to USD at 1.4057 CAD/USD (Bank of Canada, 2026-10-21)) at Peacock Lumber in Oshawa, ON; the highest was $15.00/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $9.60 vs surfaced median $11.00 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
  • 8/4 median $10.07 vs 4/4 median $9.72 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • Peacock Lumber posts prices in Canadian dollars. We converted them to US dollars at the Bank of Canada rate for October 21, 2026 (1.4057 CAD per USD); the original CAD price is in the Surfacing column, and the US-dollar figure moves with the exchange rate.
  • Only 34 prices from 9 states plus Ontario so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
