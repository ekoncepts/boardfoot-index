Hardwood lumber prices in North Carolina

We found 34 publicly posted hardwood prices from 1 North Carolina dealer, covering 9 species. Across all species and thicknesses the median is $6.20 per board foot, ranging $3.25–$14.75. Every figure below comes from a dealer's own price list or product page, checked in October 2026 — nothing is estimated.

In this data, Poplar has the lowest species median in North Carolina ($3.40/bf) and Walnut the highest ($12.20/bf). The single lowest posted price is $3.25/bf (Poplar 4/4, The Hardwood Store of NC); the highest is $14.75/bf (Walnut 12/4, The Hardwood Store of NC).
