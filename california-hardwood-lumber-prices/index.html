Hardwood lumber prices in California

We found 5 publicly posted hardwood prices from 1 California dealer, covering 5 species. Across all species and thicknesses the median is $4.29 per board foot, ranging $3.49–$7.99. Every figure below comes from a dealer's own price list or product page, checked in October 2026 — nothing is estimated.

In this data, Red Oak has the lowest species median in California ($3.49/bf) and Walnut the highest ($7.99/bf). The single lowest posted price is $3.49/bf (Red Oak 4/4, SoCalTrim); the highest is $7.99/bf (Walnut 4/4, SoCalTrim).
