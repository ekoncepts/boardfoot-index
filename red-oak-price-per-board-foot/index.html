Red Oak price per board foot

How much does red oak lumber cost? Across 36 publicly posted dealer prices checked in October 2026, red oak has a median of $5.15 per board foot, ranging $3.49–$8.32. Data comes from dealer price sheets in AZ, CA, GA, IN, MA, MI, NC, OH, VA, WA; every price links to its source.

Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $3.49/bf (4/4, S2S (13/16")) at SoCalTrim in Wildomar, CA; the highest was $8.32/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $4.55 vs surfaced median $5.75 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
  • 8/4 median $5.78 vs 4/4 median $4.12 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • Only 36 prices from 10 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
