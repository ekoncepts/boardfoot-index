Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $3.49/bf (4/4, S2S (13/16")) at SoCalTrim in Wildomar, CA; the highest was $8.32/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $4.55 vs surfaced median $5.75 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
  • 8/4 median $5.95 vs 4/4 median $4.25 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • The Knotty Lumber Co. ships from Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. Its prices here are as shown on its US storefront in US dollars (no conversion by us), and US buyers may owe import duties, tariffs or brokerage fees that are not included in the listed prices.
  • Only 38 prices from 11 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
