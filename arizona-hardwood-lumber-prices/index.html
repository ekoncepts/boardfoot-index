Hardwood lumber prices in Arizona

We found 26 publicly posted hardwood prices from 1 Arizona dealer, covering 8 species. Across all species and thicknesses the median is $9.74 per board foot, ranging $4.99–$25.99. Every figure below comes from a dealer's own price list or product page, checked in October 2026 — nothing is estimated.

In this data, Poplar has the lowest species median in Arizona ($6.49/bf) and Walnut the highest ($19.99/bf). The single lowest posted price is $4.99/bf (Poplar 4/4, Woodworkers Source); the highest is $25.99/bf (Walnut 12/4, Woodworkers Source).
