Hardwood lumber prices in Texas

We found 7 publicly posted hardwood prices from 1 Texas dealer, covering 7 species. Across all species and thicknesses the median is $14.00 per board foot, ranging $8.00–$15.00. Every figure below comes from a dealer's own price list or product page, checked in October 2026 — nothing is estimated.
