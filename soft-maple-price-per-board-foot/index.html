    Buying tips from the data

    • The lowest posted price we found was $2.99/bf (4/4, rough (Select & Better)) at Kettle Moraine Hardwoods in Hartford, WI; the highest was $10.00/bf.
    • Rough-sawn median $5.21 vs surfaced median $7.88 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
    • 8/4 median $6.75 vs 4/4 median $5.35 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
    • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
    • Peacock Lumber posts prices in Canadian dollars. We converted them to US dollars at the Bank of Canada rate for October 21, 2026 (1.4057 CAD per USD); the original CAD price is in the Surfacing column, and the US-dollar figure moves with the exchange rate.
    • Only 45 prices from 12 states plus Ontario so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
