Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $4.75/bf (4/4, rough (KD, Sel&Btr)) at Hickory & Oak Lumber in Decatur, MI; the highest was $9.00/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $5.50 vs surfaced median $5.25 in our data — rough was not cheaper here because dealers, grades and regions differ; compare like with like.
  • 8/4 median $6.75 vs 4/4 median $5.25 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • Only 24 prices from 8 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
