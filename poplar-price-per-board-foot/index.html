Poplar price per board foot

How much does poplar lumber cost? Across 59 publicly posted dealer prices checked in October 2026, poplar has a median of $4.25 per board foot, ranging $3.00–$7.99. Data comes from dealer price sheets in AZ, CA, GA, IL, IN, MA, MD, MI, NC, NJ, OH, ON, VA, WI; every price links to its source.

    Buying tips from the data

    • The lowest posted price we found was $3.00/bf (4/4) at Cygnus Lumber in Virginia Beach, VA; the highest was $7.99/bf.
    • Rough-sawn median $4.07 vs surfaced median $5.00 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
    • 8/4 median $4.97 vs 4/4 median $3.75 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
    • The Knotty Lumber Co. ships from Hamilton, Ontario, Canada. Its prices here are as shown on its US storefront in US dollars (no conversion by us), and US buyers may owe import duties, tariffs or brokerage fees that are not included in the listed prices.
    • Only 59 prices from 13 states plus Ontario so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
