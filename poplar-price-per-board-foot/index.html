Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $3.00/bf (4/4) at Cygnus Lumber in Virginia Beach, VA; the highest was $7.99/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $3.75 vs surfaced median $4.99 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
  • 8/4 median $4.97 vs 4/4 median $3.58 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Only 37 prices from 9 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
