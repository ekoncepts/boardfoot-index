Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $3.95/bf (8/4) at KenCraft Company in Toledo, OH; the highest was $9.25/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $5.95 vs surfaced median $5.75 in our data — rough was not cheaper here because dealers, grades and regions differ; compare like with like.
  • 8/4 median $6.47 vs 4/4 median $5.85 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • Only 28 prices from 10 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
