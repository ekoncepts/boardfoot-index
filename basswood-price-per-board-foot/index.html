Basswood price per board foot

    Buying tips from the data

    • The lowest posted price we found was $3.05/bf (4/4, rough, random width & length (Sel & Btr)) at L.L. Johnson Lumber in Charlotte, MI; the highest was $8.36/bf.
    • Rough-sawn median $5.25 vs surfaced median $5.85 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
    • 8/4 median $5.50 vs 4/4 median $5.16 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
    • Only 21 prices from 4 states so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
