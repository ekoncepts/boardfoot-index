Hardwood lumber prices in Georgia

We found 24 publicly posted hardwood prices from 1 Georgia dealer, covering 10 species. Across all species and thicknesses the median is $8.10 per board foot, ranging $5.35–$17.00. Every figure below comes from a dealer's own price list or product page, checked in October 2026 — nothing is estimated.

In this data, Poplar has the lowest species median in Georgia ($5.70/bf) and Walnut the highest ($16.75/bf). The single lowest posted price is $5.35/bf (Poplar 4/4, The Wood Yard); the highest is $17.00/bf (Walnut 8/4, The Wood Yard).
