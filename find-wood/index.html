    How the results are sorted

    Dealers in your state come first, then dealers whose site says they ship, then everyone else (pickup only, local delivery only, or no shipping policy found). Within each group the cheapest price per board foot is first. When a dealer posts more than one price for the same species and thickness (different grades or surfacing), we show the median of those prices and the range. If you enter a size, the estimated cost uses the same math as the board foot calculator: thickness × width × length (ft) ÷ 12 × pieces, times that dealer's price.
