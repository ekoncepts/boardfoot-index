Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $5.88/bf (4/4, S2S (13/16", sale price (was $6.99))) at SoCalTrim in Wildomar, CA; the highest was $24.99/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $9.74 vs surfaced median $12.00 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
  • 8/4 median $11.95 vs 4/4 median $8.57 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • Only 42 prices from 13 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
