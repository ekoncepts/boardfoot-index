Buying tips from the data

  • The lowest posted price we found was $4.29/bf (4/4, S2S (13/16")) at SoCalTrim in Wildomar, CA; the highest was $12.00/bf.
  • Rough-sawn median $6.95 vs surfaced median $8.50 in our data (mixed dealers and grades) — rough is cheaper per bf if you can mill it yourself.
  • 8/4 median $7.83 vs 4/4 median $5.72 per bf — thicker stock costs more per board foot here.
  • Wood Vendors prices shown are its 100–249 bf tier; that dealer lists lower per-bf prices for larger orders.
  • Only 45 prices from 14 state(s) so far — always call the dealer to confirm, and report what you pay to improve this page.
